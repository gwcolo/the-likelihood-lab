The Likelihood Lab

Maximum likelihood estimation, by feel: pick a distribution, drag its parameters, and make the observed data as probable as you can. The score is the log-likelihood — higher is better. Tip: zoom your browser out (Cmd/Ctrl and −) until the curve and the attempt panels share the screen.

Your attempts

Each panel pairs a parameter's slider with its attempt history: every release is scored — log-likelihood (vertical) against that parameter (horizontal). You can also click or drag inside the plot to set the value directly. The orange marker is your current setting; your best is the large blue circle; impossible settings (some points had probability zero) are red ✗, pinned at the floor.

All computation runs in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere. A teaching demo for maximum likelihood estimation.
